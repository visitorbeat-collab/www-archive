# Finder Entry

I am preserving this relationship provisionally.

The witness account and recovered environmental material do not share terminology, structure, or verifiable origin.

The correspondence is conceptual.

In the testimony, the witness describes being shown apparently separate phenomena:

- forest loss
- water degradation
- animal mortality
- urban activity
- industrial activity
- consumption
- disposal

The witness later describes these as parts of a single interconnected process.

That resembles the organizing principle used in `environmental_observation_001`.

It is not evidence that the testimony and recovered document originate from the same source.

The resemblance may be coincidental.

It may also reflect the witness imposing coherence onto an otherwise fragmented experience.

One detail remains difficult to dismiss.

The witness does not describe being told what the images meant.

The interpretation appears to occur after the experience.

This distinction corresponds with a recurring separation elsewhere in the Index:

**observation**

**interpretation**

I am retaining the relationship at low confidence.

For now:

**correspondence observed**
