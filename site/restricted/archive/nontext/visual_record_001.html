REPRESENTATION

This is a human photographic record of a terrestrial event. The camera position, exposure, framing, and selected moment determine what remains visible.

The photograph records light within one bounded field. It does not contain the full spatial extent, duration, cause, ecological function, human consequence, or later recovery of the fire.

INTERPRETATION CONSTRAINT

The image alone does not establish ignition source, preventability, total severity, or responsibility. Wildfire may function as ecological process, destructive event, or both at different boundaries and timescales.

Visible flame should not be treated as the complete event. Effects may persist outside the frame through smoke, habitat change, displacement, atmospheric transport, and regeneration.

This object is retained as media, not classified as a population event within the Evidentiary Corpus.
