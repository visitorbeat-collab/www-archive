relational_reconstruction_001

status: provisional classification: constructed model

I made this diagram because I kept losing the relationships when I wrote them out as a sequence.

It is not recovered material.

That distinction needs to stay visible.

The recovered fragments repeatedly describe boundaries becoming less useful as relationships extend beyond them. I wanted to see whether that still held when the relationships were placed together instead of listed one after another.

So I assigned anonymous nodes, separated the connections into a few explicit types, and arranged them for legibility. Every visible position is therefore my decision. So is the number of clusters. So is the appearance of the pathways.

The diagram demonstrates a possibility. It does not preserve the appearance of anything I found.

a useful reconstruction is still a reconstruction

The part I wanted to test was simple: a structure can look peripheral when the boundary is narrow and become consequential when the boundary expands. The model does show that. It also shows how easily a diagram can make an interpretation feel more authoritative than the material supporting it.

That may be the more important result.

I am keeping the diagram with my working notes rather than with the retained objects. It records how I tried to understand the material, not what the material originally was.
