Why This Exists

date: undetermined status: retained

If you're reading these notes, you've already put more effort into this than I expected anyone else to.

They weren't written for an audience. Most began as reminders to myself: where I found something, what caught my attention, why I thought it might matter, and what ordinary explanation I had considered before deciding to keep it.

I had no interest in proving that something extraordinary happened to me. For a long time I was more interested in proving that nothing extraordinary had happened at all, and there are still days when I would prefer that explanation.

The notes helped because memory did not. If I wrote down what I thought at the time, I could return later and see whether a connection had actually been present or whether I had quietly added it after learning something new.

For a while that was enough. Then the collection grew, and I began finding relationships between material I had filed months or years apart. I couldn't keep those connections in my head. Eventually the notes became almost as difficult to navigate as the things they were supposed to explain.

That's why I built the Index.

Originally it was an organizational tool. At least, that's how I understood it at the time.

Most of what is here proves nothing on its own. Some of it is probably irrelevant. Some may be wrong, and there are records I would classify differently now. I've kept many of those mistakes because removing them would make the structure look obvious from the beginning.

It wasn't.

What happened was slower and less satisfying. Individual explanations became less convincing, while certain relationships became harder to dismiss. I know those two developments can be confused. Losing confidence in one explanation does not make another explanation true.

That distinction is why these notes still exist. They are not proof, and they are not a finished account. They are the record of what I noticed, what I got wrong, and why I kept going when it would have been easier to stop.
