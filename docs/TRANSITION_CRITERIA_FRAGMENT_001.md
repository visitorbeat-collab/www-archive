# Key Revelation

The recovered fragment introduces:

> capability threshold

and

> reliability threshold

These are not the same threshold.

A civilization may pass one and fail the other.

This directly undermines the assumption that technological sophistication alone determines readiness for contact.

---

# Critical Phrase

> Capability is insufficient. Reliability must precede transfer.

This should become one of the most important lines in the archive.

It should remain unexplained.

---

# Why "Transfer"

The Observers would likely conceptualize contact partly in terms of transfer because contact changes access.

It may involve transfer of:

- information
- methods
- technologies
- relationships
- navigation
- broader system awareness
- participation rights
- consequence capacity

Using "transfer" is therefore more operational than "contact."

The Finder may later interpret it as contact.
