OBSERVATION

The source object contains no acoustic recording. It consists of measurements associated with forty particle-flow candidates inside one simulated Higgs-to-bottom event retained in a human research dataset.

The audible object was produced by moving selected spatial and energetic relationships into a frequency range available to human hearing. Nothing in the source establishes that this is what the event sounded like. The event did not occur in an atmosphere, and the translation should not be mistaken for recovered physical sound.

Every audible feature depends upon a human-selected correspondence. Sequence, pitch, amplitude, stereo position, and timbre are interpretive decisions applied after measurement.
