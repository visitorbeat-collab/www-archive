related: environmental_observation_001 status: retained

I kept coming back to this file because I couldn't quite understand why it belonged with the rest of the material.

At first I filed it mentally as environmental content and moved on. That seemed reasonable enough.

The document appears to begin with a decline in something roughly analogous to a pollinator population, then follows changes in plant reproduction, food availability, cultivated production, and eventually the response of a technological population.

At the time, none of that struck me as especially relevant to the questions I was working on.

I think that assumption is probably why I missed what was interesting about it.

Every time I reread the document, the subject seemed to change.

It starts with P-17, then moves into plant reproduction, then the surrounding ecological network, cultivated food production, technological response, and eventually back out into the wider ecological system.

If I had been organizing the material from scratch, I probably would have split those into several different subjects.

Biology.
Agriculture.
Technology.
Population behavior.
Environmental change.

Whoever produced the record apparently didn't make those divisions, or at least not in the way I expected.

The document treats the changes as parts of a continuous process rather than as separate subjects that happen to affect one another.

That was what sent me back to classification_schema_v1.

The primary observational unit is the interacting system.

I had read that line before and assumed it mostly meant "include more context."

I don't think that's quite enough anymore.

The problem may be that the categories I use to organize things are introducing boundaries that aren't actually present in the source material.

I've spent a lot of time asking what individual documents are about.

I'm starting to think that can be misleading.

A more useful question here might be:

What process is this document observing?

If I'm reading it correctly, environmental_observation_001 is not really about P-17 in isolation.

P-17 is simply where the larger process becomes easiest to notice.

What follows is not a chain of unrelated topics. It is one system changing through several connected states.

That sounds obvious written out this way, which makes me slightly suspicious of it.

Still, it explains the structure of the document better than my original classification did.

For now I'm leaving the environmental label in place.

Partly because it is still useful, and partly because I want to preserve the fact that I initially misunderstood what I was looking at.
