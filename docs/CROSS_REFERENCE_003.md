# Finder Entry

I am retaining this relationship at moderate confidence.

The witness account cannot be verified.

No evidence establishes that the experience described in `testimony_002` originated from the same source as the recovered assessments.

The resemblance is nevertheless more specific than the relationship preserved in `cross_reference_002`.

The witness describes:

- being presented with multiple possible outcomes
- attempting to identify a correct intervention
- encountering consequences that vary across time and scale
- recognizing that the relevant system was larger than initially assumed
- withholding action after recognizing insufficient understanding

This sequence resembles several assessment variables appearing elsewhere in the Index.

Most notably:

**uncertainty**

**system boundary**

**decision threshold**

**restraint**

In `decision_threshold_assessment_001`, the relevant standard is not whether uncertainty can be eliminated.

It is whether unresolved uncertainty changes the threshold required before consequential action proceeds.

In `technological_population_assessment_002`, restraint includes delay, reduced intervention, testing, and deliberate non-use of available capability.

The witness describes no comparable terminology.

The behavioral sequence is similar.

That distinction matters.

The strongest correspondence occurs at the point where the witness stops asking:

**Which choice is correct?**

and instead recognizes:

**I don't know enough.**

The apparent result is withheld action.

I cannot determine whether this was:

- the intended purpose of the experience
- a response produced by the witness independently
- a later reconstruction of fragmented memory
- coincidence

The subsequent expansion of the witness's perceived system boundary is also notable.

The witness begins by evaluating one location.

Only after withholding judgment do they report noticing connections extending beyond it.

This resembles a pattern already present elsewhere:

**insufficient model**

→

**recognition of uncertainty**

→

**boundary expansion**

→

**revised decision condition**

I am not classifying the testimony as evidence of assessment.

For now:

**structural correspondence: moderate**

**common origin: unresolved**
