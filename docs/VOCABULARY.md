# VOCABULARY.md

**Status:** Internal Language Reference
**Applies to:** Observer documents only.

---

# Purpose

Observer language should communicate an unfamiliar worldview without requiring an unfamiliar language.

The reader should understand every sentence.

The alien quality should emerge from **what the Observers choose to describe**, not from invented words.

The Observers classify systems before individuals.

Processes before events.

Relationships before objects.

Outcomes before intentions.

---

# Fundamental Rule

Observers rarely describe what something *is*.

They describe **what function it performs within a larger system.**

# Art

This category is important.

Observers appear deeply interested in art because it compresses enormous amounts of cultural information into symbolic form.

Prefer:

symbolic cognitive artifact

or

temporal acoustic pattern encoding

cooperative symbolic transmission

# Language

Observers appear less interested in vocabulary than in information transfer.

Language is therefore often described as:

shared symbolic encoding system

# Memory

# Consciousness

The Observers deliberately avoid defining consciousness absolutely.

Instead they refer to:

active cognitive state

or

subjective processing framework

This suggests the question remains scientifically open even for them.

---

# Intelligence

Observers distinguish between knowledge and intelligence.

Knowledge is accumulation.

Intelligence is adaptive interpretation.

Advanced intelligence requires:

* model revision
* consequence prediction
* uncertainty tolerance
* systemic reasoning

---

# Observation

Observation is data acquisition.

Nothing more.

---

# Interpretation

Interpretation is the construction of predictive models from incomplete information.

Interpretation represents the defining capability of mature civilizations.

---

# Contact

Observers never describe first contact as a celebration.

Prefer:

developmental transition

or

cross-civilizational integration

---

# Failure

Observers do not appear embarrassed by failure.

Failed predictions become valuable data.

Failure therefore represents:

model improvement opportunity

---

# Uncertainty

One of the most important Observer concepts.

Uncertainty is never weakness.

Uncertainty simply identifies where additional observation is required.

Observer documents should therefore frequently contain statements such as:

Prediction confidence reduced.

Additional observation recommended.

Current model insufficient.

Existing explanation incomplete.

---

Avoid unless quoting humans:

good

deserve

justice

innocent

monster

miracle

fate

destiny

chosen

They would almost certainly recognize these concepts, but they would classify them rather than adopt them as analytical terms.

---

# Closing Principle

The Observers do not experience the world as a collection of separate objects.

They experience it as a network of interacting systems.

Their language should constantly remind the reader of that difference without ever needing to state it directly.
