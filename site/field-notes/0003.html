Coincidence

created: various revised: various status: retained

I've never been completely satisfied with the way people use the word "coincidence."

Sometimes it really is the best description available. Two things happen near each other, they look related, and there is no good reason to think they are.

But calling something a coincidence can also sound more explanatory than it actually is.

Most of the time it just means I don't know whether the relationship is meaningful.

That distinction became important once I started working on the Index, because a lot of what first caught my attention looked exactly like coincidence.

Some of it probably was.

Other things became harder to dismiss only because the same kinds of relationships kept appearing in places I hadn't originally connected.

The problem is that both situations can look identical at the beginning.

I've become suspicious of the impulse to settle the question too quickly in either direction.

Dismissing something immediately because it sounds improbable is easy. Deciding it must be meaningful because it feels improbable is just as easy.

Neither approach gets me very far.

So I try to leave coincidence available as an explanation for as long as it still works, without using the word to close the question before I've actually examined it.

I'm better at that some days than others.
