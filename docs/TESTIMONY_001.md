# TESTIMONY

I don't know exactly when it started.

That's one of the things that bothers me most.

I remember being awake.

I remember getting out of bed.

After that there are pieces.

I was somewhere bright, but I don't remember entering a room.

The light didn't seem to come from anywhere specific.

There were figures around me.

I could not see their faces clearly.

I remember being frightened, but I don't remember them reacting to that.

They seemed busy.

Not aggressive.

Not comforting either.

It felt like being examined.

That is the closest word I have.

At some point the room disappeared.

Or maybe I stopped seeing it.

I suddenly had the impression that I was very high above the ground.

I could see the planet.

I know how that sounds.

I could see weather systems and darkness and the curve of the atmosphere.

Then the view changed.

I saw areas of forest disappearing.

Not one forest.

Many.

I saw water turning colors.

I saw dead fish.

I saw large areas where the ground looked dry and damaged.

There were fires.

Then cities.

Very large cities.

Roads.

Lights.

Industrial areas.

It happened too quickly for me to understand what I was looking at.

The images didn't feel random.

They seemed connected.

That was the part that frightened me.

I had the feeling I was being shown what we were doing.

I remember thinking:

**They want us to stop.**

I don't remember anyone saying that.

I want to be clear about that.

There was no voice telling me we were destroying the planet.

I just understood it that way.

Then I saw something I still have difficulty explaining.

The images became less recognizable.

I saw something that looked like a network.

Not a computer network.

More like veins.

Everything seemed connected to everything else.

Forests.

Water.

Animals.

Cities.

People.

Something would change in one part and then something else changed far away.

I couldn't follow it.

I remember becoming overwhelmed.

There was too much happening at once.

I wanted the images to stop.

Then I saw people again.

Crowds.

Traffic.

Buildings.

Stores.

Screens.

People buying things.

People throwing things away.

Nothing unusual.

That was somehow worse than the fires.

Nobody seemed to know anything was happening.

I remember thinking that we were behaving like the planet was something outside of us.

Like we lived on top of it.

Not inside it.

That thought stayed with me afterward.

I don't remember being returned.

The next clear memory I have is sitting on the edge of my bed.

It was still dark.

For several days I felt completely certain that I had been shown a warning.

I'm less certain now.

I know memory changes.

I know dreams can feel real.

I also know that once you interpret something, the interpretation starts changing the memory.

So I don't know whether they showed me that we were destroying the planet.

That is what I thought it meant.

Maybe that is not the same thing.

# Internal Design Notes

## Purpose

Testimony 001 introduces the first human account of apparent contact with the phenomenon.

Its primary function is to demonstrate the difference between:

**observation**

and

**interpretation**

The witness genuinely experiences something.

The witness then assigns human meaning to it.

The account must never establish that the witness's interpretation is objectively correct.

# Important Ambiguity

The witness believes:

> They were warning humanity about environmental destruction.

That interpretation should remain plausible.

However, later material may support a more nuanced possibility:

The experience was not necessarily a message whose semantic content was:

> Stop damaging Earth.

It may instead have been an observational or cognitive procedure testing whether the subject could recognize:

* system interdependence
* delayed consequence
* ecological dependency
* technological population influence
* causal relationships across scale

The witness converts that experience into the nearest available human moral framework:

**a warning.**

Both interpretations may remain possible.

---

# Why This Matters

The project should repeatedly distinguish between:

**the experience**

and

**the witness's model of the experience**

This allows testimony to remain valuable without requiring the archive to endorse every conclusion contained within it.

Human testimony can therefore be emotionally truthful while epistemically incomplete.
