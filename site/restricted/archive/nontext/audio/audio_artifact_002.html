OBSERVATION

The retained object contains acoustic activity produced by one or more humpback whales and recorded by an anchored hydrophone near the mouth of Glacier Bay. Vocal activity occupies much of the record. Breathing and wheeze blows are audible near its end.

The record was produced without a human performer and without translation into a human musical system. Human decisions remain present in hydrophone placement, recording, selection, naming, encoding, and retention.

INTERPRETATION CONSTRAINT

Pattern establishes neither message nor intended recipient. Repetition, variation, and response may be described without assigning human language, music, or motive to the source organism.

The object demonstrates that consequential acoustic structure is not exclusive to human production. It does not establish what any individual signal meant within the source environment.

PROVENANCE

This is a documented human field recording incorporated during reconstruction. It is not presented as audio recovered directly from the source system.
