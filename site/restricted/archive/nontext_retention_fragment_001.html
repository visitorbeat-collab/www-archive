REPRESENTATION LOSS

Not all retained material should be converted into semantic reconstruction.

Translation into language introduces selection.

Selection introduces interpretation.

Interpretation may preserve relationship.

It may also remove it.

Some source structures contain information in:

duration
interval
repetition
spatial relation
frequency
phase
sequence
absence
distortion
transition

These properties may be reduced or destroyed when translated into descriptive language.

RETENTION CONDITION

Where interpretation materially changes the object under evaluation, retain the object.

Do not replace it with explanation.

A description of a signal is not the signal.

A description of an image is not the image.

A transcription of a sound is not the sound.

A diagram reconstructed from memory is not equivalent to the relationship originally represented.

CLASSIFICATION

Non-semantic material should be retained separately where:

translation confidence is low
structure may contain independent information
compression removes potentially relevant relationships
interpretation is likely to dominate observation
later analytical methods may recover additional structure
