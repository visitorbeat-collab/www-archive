## PARTICIPATION CONDITION

Observation of a developing population is not equivalent to preparation for participation.

Participation changes the observed system.

The observing population becomes part of the developmental environment.

This introduces reciprocal consequence.

---

Access should not be expanded according to demonstrated acquisition capacity.

Acquisition capacity predicts only:

**ability to obtain**

It does not predict:

**ability to regulate possession**

---

A population may successfully infer the existence of an external system while remaining unsuitable for direct inclusion within it.

Recognition is not qualification.

Detection is not qualification.

Communication is not qualification.

Understanding is not qualification.

---

The relevant condition is not whether the population can cross the boundary.

The relevant condition is whether the boundary can be widened without producing unacceptable consequence.

### PARTICIPATION

Participation is not granted as compensation for developmental achievement.

Participation is permitted where expected consequence becomes tolerable.

This distinction should be maintained.

---

The developmental population may interpret deferred inclusion as:

- punishment
- concealment
- superiority
- exclusion
- hostility

These interpretations are expected.

They do not alter the condition.

---

If a population is instructed:

**demonstrate responsibility toward systems beyond direct utility**

subsequent compliance cannot distinguish:

**independent regulation**

from:

**behavior performed for access**

Initial contact should not provide sufficient information to convert uncertainty into authority.

The population must remain able to:

- disagree
- refuse
- investigate
- revise
- reject incorrect external claims
- identify external error

It can only comply.
