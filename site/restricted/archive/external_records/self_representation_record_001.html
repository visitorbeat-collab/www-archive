SELF-REPRESENTATION ARTIFACT 001

Deliberate population-originated representation intended for interpretation by unidentified nonlocal intelligence.

SOURCE IDENTIFICATION

Voyager 1 / Voyager 2 carried media artifact.

Local designation: Voyager Golden Record.

HUMAN CLASSIFICATION

Interstellar message.

Cultural and biological record.

Representation of originating world and population prepared for possible discovery by nonhuman intelligence.

EXTERNAL CLASSIFICATION

Self-representation artifact.

Deliberate legibility attempt under unknown recipient conditions.

OBSERVED FUNCTION

The originating population constructed a bounded representation of its biosphere, anatomy, environment, technical capability, language, symbolic systems, and cultural production.

The object was designed to remain interpretable after separation from the population that produced it.

Recipient identity was not known at time of construction.

TRANSMISSION CONDITION

Recipient biology unknown.

Recipient sensory architecture unknown.

Recipient symbolic framework unknown.

Recipient social organization unknown.

Probability of interception not established.

Probability of successful interpretation not established.

RETENTION BASIS

The artifact was deliberately prepared for interpretation by an intelligence outside the originating population.

The selection therefore preserves evidence not only of what the population knew, but of what it considered appropriate to reveal about itself.

Omission is not independently interpretable as concealment.

Selection remains informative regardless of intent.

ASSESSMENT NOTE

Finite representation requires exclusion.

Exclusion creates a boundary between the population and the representation of the population.

The artifact therefore cannot be treated as a neutral sample.

It is a constructed interface between an unknown recipient and the population's own model of itself.

Selection is part of the transmission.

REPRESENTATIONAL ASYMMETRY

The originating population supplied information about its environment, body structure, communication, technical activity, and cultural production without possessing equivalent information about the intended recipient.

The exchange was therefore unilateral.

The population attempted to become legible before determining what legibility might produce.

INTERPRETIVE CAUTION

The artifact should not be treated as a complete description of the originating population.

It records a decision about representation under conditions of radical uncertainty.

The distinction is material.

The transmitted object does not describe the population as it exists.

It describes the population as it elected to become legible to an unknown other.
