post_exposure_observation_fragment_001

protocol: cognitive / interpretive stage: post-exposure confidence: moderate

[BEGIN RECOVERED SECTION]

Immediate assessment performance is insufficient for developmental classification.

Subject behavior during exposure may reflect:

temporary adaptation
situational compliance
novelty response
externally structured attention
short-duration model revision

These conditions do not establish persistent integration.

Post-exposure observation should determine whether interpretive changes remain operative after removal of the assessment environment.

Relevant variables include:

spontaneous relational reasoning
independent boundary revision
tolerance of unresolved uncertainty
delayed consequence recognition
model correction without external prompting
transfer of relational reasoning between domains

Do not require conscious recognition of prior exposure.

Structural integration may persist without episodic recall.

Where relational reasoning is observed only in direct proximity to exposure:

temporary adaptation

Where relational reasoning reappears independently under unrelated conditions:

possible structural retention

Where equivalent reasoning persists across domains and extended interval:

integration candidate

[SECTION MISSING]

Post-exposure observation should minimize interference.

Direct reinforcement of prior assessment content may alter the variable being measured.

Where possible:

do not identify assessment purpose
do not confirm subject interpretation
do not provide explanatory framework
do not reward reproduction of prior patterns
do not accelerate model formation artificially

Observation should determine what the subject constructs without correction.

Subject-generated models may differ substantially from original exposure structure.

Surface mismatch does not independently indicate failed retention.

Assess:

relational equivalence
rather than
representational similarity

Example:

A subject may reconstruct equivalent causal organization using:

language
diagrams
classification systems
symbolic artifacts
social models
ecological models
technological models

Expression medium is secondary.

Relational organization is primary.

[SECTION MISSING]

Where subject begins independently collecting material according to relational rather than categorical similarity:

continue observation.

Where subject reorganizes previously unrelated material according to common process structure:

continue observation.

Where subject revises its own classification model following contradiction:

continue observation.

Where subject preserves uncertainty rather than forcing closure:

continue observation.

These behaviors do not independently confirm structural retention.

Convergence across multiple behaviors increases confidence.

Long-duration observation is necessary where:

subject environment changes substantially
social context changes
available information increases
explanatory frameworks become available
subject gains greater technical capability
subject encounters contradictory evidence

Persistence under changed conditions carries greater predictive value.

[SECTION MISSING]

Do not assume that subject construction reflects intentional reproduction of assessment material.

The subject may experience reconstruction as:

intuition
familiarity
independent discovery
pattern recognition
methodological preference

Conscious source attribution is not required.

Where subject-generated relational model produces access to previously unresolved structural correspondences:

flag for reassessment.

Reassessment should determine whether:

subject is reconstructing exposure
or
subject is independently converging upon equivalent structure

Distinction may remain unresolved.

Observation duration:
indeterminate

Termination condition:
sufficient confidence in persistence classification
